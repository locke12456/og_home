Starting today, we'll be posting regular updates on the development of *The Reincarnated*.

This is where we'll share the journey: the thinking behind new systems, art progress, the pitfalls we hit and the bugs we fixed, and every milestone along the way.

## What to expect

- **Systems**: how rebirth, gifts, the daily lives of residents and the economy grow step by step
- **Art**: progress on character portraits, the interface and environments
- **Fixes**: the more interesting bugs, and how we caught them
- **Milestones**: playable builds, events and release news

> A blessing, or a curse? The fate of the reincarnated is in your hands.

You can follow updates via [RSS](/en/devlog/feed.xml).
